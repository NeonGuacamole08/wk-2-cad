# Making the Attack Ring 
Start by making 3 circles on your top plane. You can access your top plane by clicking on N. Then go to Sketch.
<img width="442" height="53" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea57b425-977f-40f0-931d-2da4045b67bc"/>

 You will have to select a sketch plane to choose your tool. Select the top plane.
And use the center point circle tool to make your circles well 

<img width="329" height="93" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7515b624-0e57-4a10-8351-ccad6a2d4cf9"/>

Give your circles the following measurements. To change the measurements of your circle, click on the dimensions tool, select your circles, and place the appropriate dimensions. 
<img width="70" height="68" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca18c793-068a-44ef-97b8-a1525626a338"/>
