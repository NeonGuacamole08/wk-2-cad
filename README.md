# Making the Attack Ring General Template  

<img width="732" height="492" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-18 181356" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b9d9326-04ac-478e-bd3a-5df54bf65ffd" />



Start by making 3 circles on your top plane. To access your top plane, press N, then go to Sketch.


 Select a sketch plane to choose your tool. Select the top plane.
And use the center point circle tool to make your circles well 



Give your circles the following measurements. To change the measurements of your circle, click on the dimensions tool, select your circles, and place the appropriate dimensions. 
<img width="70" height="68" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca18c793-068a-44ef-97b8-a1525626a338"/>

Then we have to create construction lines. These lines create intersections that section different parts of our Beyblade. It should look like this

Then you need to define specific angles!
Start by selecting the diagonal line and the center line by just clicking on them like so:
<img width="398" height="283" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/115c3dcf-60ce-49b7-8118-7c385196c840" />
Then set the angle to 45 degrees and do the same on the other side. 


Then, to do the same on the other side using the mirror tool:
<img width="41" height="53" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e417ea83-2a1f-4ded-939b-06ffee6fcdea" />


Click on the horizontal line:
<img width="791" height="372" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0b384de-99b1-4027-8f65-3e8ba3112c20" />


No need to set the angles to 45 because the lines were mirrored and thus have the same measurements. 

Then use your trim tool to get rid of the lines.

Then click on the extruded face and, using Sketch,  start a rectangle from the center. The rectangle should measure 0.128 cm.

Add a circle, 
<img width="707" height="284" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-17 004236" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b8fb5d8-8d6c-4db9-b069-4f4f5b/>

 Use the project tool 
 between the circle we just created and the edge of our base.de6935"
If it's done correctly, clicking on the bottom of the base should result in this blue line appearing

Finally, use trim to remove the top of the rectangle and the 3 other sides of the circle. Then use Sketch Fillet to round the corners of our new shape. 
You should be left with this. <img width="560" height="206" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-16 234832" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c39207ad-a365-4974-b064-824ef9772319" />

Extrude this at a thickness of 0.1 cm. 

Then we need to hollow out the shape we just made. To do that (STILL WITHIN SKETCH 3), we use Offset and select these sides. <img width="547" height="233" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/52779946-f76c-480a-85dd-46d3484b4458"/>

Pull it inward like this. 

Make another circle.


 Position it as so (the tutorial I followed suggested putting a distance of 1.5 mm; instead, I put one of 1.7 (0.17cm) because I thought it was too close. 
<img width="879" height="441" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-17 000922" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b9922448-1b30-4b74-b875-88495c0cb66c" />
Then select the innermost side of the shape and the circle we just made to link them closer


Finally, drag the small Sketch you made to the circle so that they touch like this. 
<img width="865" height="461" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-17 000810" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8c36f1a-6bae-4b5b-9629-d1c31d28d9b2" />
Then use the Trim tool to remove the parts of the circle I didn't need.


Then extrude this shape you have just made to REMOVE. Select a second ending to extrude on both sides. Finally, extrude with a thickness of 0.0025cm. 


FINALLY, use the fillet tool to round ONLY the outer corners and the top of the extruded shape. 

Then you're done with the first half.
